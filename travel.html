ItemDetails
Conference Venue University of Technology Sydney (UTS)
City Campus, Ultimo, NSW 2007, Australia
Primary Buildings UTS Building 8 (UTS Business School)Main Sessions
Exact room allocations will be announced in the Program.
From Sydney Airport (SYD) Airport Train (T8) to Central Station (~15 min), then 8–12 min walk to UTS.
Taxi/Rideshare ~20–35 min depending on traffic.
Public Transport Opal card/contactless payments accepted across train, light rail, bus, and ferry.
Nearest stops: Central Station (Train) · Haymarket/Chinatown (Light Rail).
Parking Limited on-street and commercial parking in Ultimo/Haymarket. Public transport is recommended.
Accessibility Step-free access and lifts available in UTS buildings; please indicate requirements during registration.

Tip: Allow extra time during weekday peaks (07:00–09:30 and 16:30–18:30).

TopicDetails
Climate (July) Cool and mild. Typical highs ~16-18 °C, lows ~8–10 °C. Showers possible-bring a light jacket.
Time Zone Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST), UTC+10.
Power Type I power plugs (230 V, 50 Hz). Bring an adapter if required.
Mobile & Internet Prepaid eSIM/SIM widely available (Telstra, Optus, Vodafone). Free Wi-Fi on campus for attendees.
Safety Generally safe; observe usual city precautions. In an emergency, dial 000.
Attractions Sydney Opera House, Harbour Bridge, The Rocks, Darling Harbour, Bondi & Manly Beaches, Royal Botanic Garden.

More local tips will be shared closer to the conference dates.
