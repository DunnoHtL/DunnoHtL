Call for Papers

FLINS-ISKE 2026 welcomes original research contributions related to theoretical analysis, algorithm development, and practical applications in computational intelligence. The conference particularly encourages submissions in computational intelligence, intelligent systems, and knowledge engineering. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

Fundamental Theories & Methodologies
  • Fuzzy Rule-Based Machine Learning
  • Enhancing Machine Learning Interpretability in AI Decision Making
  • Machine Learning Systems for Robust Decision Making under Uncertainty
  • Fuzzy Cognitive Maps and Deep Learning: Modeling Human-like Decision Processes
Algorithm & Model Optimization
  • Fuzzy Clustering in Unsupervised Learning: Improving Group Decision Making
  • Deep Neural Networks for Adaptive Decision Making in Dynamic Environments
  • Multi-Objective Optimization with ML: Balancing Trade-offs in Decision Spaces
Applications & Case Studies
  • ML-Enhanced Fuzzy Control Systems for Autonomous Vehicles
  • Machine Learning-based Real-Time Decision Making
  • Medical AI: Supporting Diagnosis and Treatment Decision Making
  • Financial Decision Making with Fuzzy ML Models
  • AI for Energy Management: Optimizing Decision Making in Smart Grids
Challenges & Frontiers
  • Explainable AI: Addressing Black Box Decision Making
  • Ethical Decision Making: Mitigating Bias in Autonomous Systems
  • Human-in-the-Loop AI: Collaborative Decision Making in Complex Systems
  • Reinforcement Learning: Adaptive Decision Making in Robotics
  • Fuzzy ML for Climate Modeling: Supporting Policy Decision Making under Uncertainty
