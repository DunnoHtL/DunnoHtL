Submission Guidelines

Authors must submit their papers via the FLINS-ISKE 2026 OpenReview account (please specify the relevant Special Session during submission, if applicable). All papers must be written in English and formatted using the LaTeX templates of Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS). DO NOT use Word templates. The review process will be SINGLE-BLIND.

Full paper submission: Researchers are invited to submit a full paper (written in English only) with a minimum length of 12 pages (excluding references) by March 1, 2026. All accepted full papers will be published in the Springer LNCS proceedings. For accepted papers, both oral and poster presentations are permitted.

Abstract-only submission: Researchers are invited to submit an extended abstract of up to 2 pages (written in English only) by March 1, 2026. The extended abstract should include the title, authors, affiliations, abstract, keywords, the main body of the extended abstract, and key references. For accepted abstract-only submissions, both oral and poster presentations are permitted.
