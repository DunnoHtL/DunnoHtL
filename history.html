Conference History

FLINS

FLINS 2026 is the 17th edition in a series of conferences on applied computational intelligence.

Past editions: Mol (1994, 1996), Antwerp (1998), Bruges (2000), Ghent (2002), Blankenburg (2004), Genova (2006), Madrid (2008), Chengdu (2010), Istanbul (2012), Paraíba (2014), Roubaix (2016), Belfast (2018), Cologne (2020), Tianjin (2022), Madrid (2024).

ISKE

ISKE 2026 is the 21st edition in a prestigious series on intelligent systems and knowledge engineering.

Past editions: Shanghai (2006), Chengdu (2007), Xiamen (2008), Hasselt (2009), Hangzhou (2010), Shanghai (2011), Beijing (2012), Shenzhen (2013), Paraíba (2014), Taipei (2015), Roubaix (2016), Nanjing (2017), Belfast (2018), Dalian (2019), Cologne (2020), Chengdu (2021), Tianjin (2022), Fuzhou (2023), Madrid (2024), Shunde (2025).
