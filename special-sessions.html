Call for Special Sessions

Researchers interested in organizing special sessions are invited to submit their proposals to FLINS-ISKE 2026 by contacting the Session Chairs via email. Session Chairs will be responsible for attracting submissions and managing the review process for their respective sessions. Each special session must have a minimum of FOUR accepted papers; otherwise, it may be cancelled or merged with another session.

Proposal Requirements

  • Session title
  • Brief description of scope and motivation (50-100 words)
  • Names and contact information (emails) of organisers
  • Brief biographical details of organisers (50-100 words per organiser)

Please send the required information to flins.iske@gmail.com to submit your proposal.
